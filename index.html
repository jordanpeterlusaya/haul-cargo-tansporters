How it works

Three steps to live cargo orders

Built for transport companies that need clear coverage, fast offers, and one place to run the job.

  1. 01

    Register your company

    Business email, notification phone, and company profile — then Haul verifies your account.

  2. 02

    Choose operating regions

    Serve all of Tanzania or pick exact regions. You only see orders that match your coverage.

  3. 03

    Accept and move cargo

    Review offers, accept eligible jobs, track in transit, and close completed deliveries.

Features

Only what your ops team needs

No clutter. The portal mirrors live Haul cargo data — same orders Agents already use.

  • Region-matched offers Orders filtered by your selected Tanzania regions or nationwide coverage.
  • Available → accepted → transit Clear queues for new offers, active jobs, in-transit freight, and completed work.
  • Order detail & directions Cargo photos, pickup and drop-off, contacts when authorized, maps in one view.
  • SMS + in-portal alerts Company phone notified on new eligible orders, with unread counts in the dashboard.
  • Drivers & vehicles Keep fleet and dispatcher contacts aligned with daily operations.
  • Company profile Update regions, notification preferences, and verification documents anytime.
